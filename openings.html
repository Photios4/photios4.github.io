Job Opportunities

I am always interested in recruiting motivated and talented candidates who want to pursue a PhD on topics related to my research interests. Postdoctoral researchers with a strong mathematical background able to conduct independent research in communications and/or control are encouraged to apply.

Open PhD Position at EURECOM: Reinforcement Learning, Information Theory and Decentralized Control

We invite applications for a funded, three-year PhD position focusing on theoretical foundations and scalable algorithms for learning and decision-making in distributed systems. Research directions include reinforcement learning, information theory, decentralized control, and uncertainty quantification for reliable and secure operation.

For example, the research could investigate how distributed sensors and controllers learn decision policies, exchange information over limited communication links, and use uncertainty estimates to improve reliability and resilience. The precise direction will be shaped by the candidate's background and interests.

The research will be conducted at EURECOM with Prof. Photios A. Stavrou and Prof. Matteo Zecchin, with the PhD degree awarded by Sorbonne Université. EURECOM is a leading French graduate engineering school (Grande École) and research centre with internationally recognised faculty. Located in Sophia Antipolis on the Côte d'Azur, it offers an international research environment alongside the vibrant cultural life and outdoor opportunities of the French Riviera.

Candidate profile: Applicants should hold, or be completing, a Master's degree in a relevant discipline, have strong mathematical skills, and demonstrate an interest in theoretical research. Knowledge of reinforcement learning, information theory, stochastic control or statistical learning is particularly welcome.

How to apply: Please email Photios A. Stavrou and Matteo Zecchin with a detailed CV, academic transcripts, contact details of at least two referees, and a one-page research statement. Use the subject line “PhD Application - Learning Information and Control”.

Deadline: Applications will be reviewed until the position is filled. Starting date: To be agreed with the selected candidate. Informal enquiries are welcome.
